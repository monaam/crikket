Install the Trybe Feedback extension

Send screenshots and screen recordings of our products straight to the product team. Setup takes about 5 minutes and you only do it once.

You need: Google Chrome (or Edge/Brave) and a Trybe Feedback account. You'll have received an invitation or a "Your Trybe Feedback account is ready" email.

1Download and unzip

Download the extension (.zip)
  1. Unzip it: double-click it on Mac, or right-click → Extract All on Windows.
  2. Move the unzipped folder chrome-mv3 somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder.

Don't delete or move this folder later, or the extension stops working.

2Add it to Chrome

  1. In the address bar, type chrome://extensions and press Enter.
  2. Turn on Developer mode (switch in the top-right corner).
  3. Click Load unpacked (top-left).
  4. Select the chrome-mv3 folder from step 1.
  5. Crikket now shows up in the list.
  6. Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 next to the address bar, then the pin 📌 next to Crikket so its icon is always visible.

Chrome may sometimes show a banner about "developer mode extensions". That's expected; just close it.

3Sign in

  1. Go to feedback.thetrybe.xyz.
  2. Invited by email? Click Sign up and create your account with the same email address the invitation was sent to. Enter the code you receive to verify it.
  3. Got a "Your account is ready" email instead? Click Forgot password? and choose a password with the code you receive.
  4. Stay signed in. The extension uses this same login.

Sign up only works for invited email addresses. Ask the product team if you haven't been invited.

4Pick the right workspace before you capture

Reports are filed under the workspace (Fatoura, Trybe, ComptaLégal or Tutorios) that is currently selected on the website. The extension has no workspace picker.

  • If you only belong to one workspace, skip this step.
  • If you belong to several, open feedback.thetrybe.xyz, switch workspace with the selector at the top left, then capture.

A report filed in the wrong workspace won't be seen by the right team.

5Send feedback

  1. Open the page with the problem and click the Crikket icon.
  2. Choose one:
    • Screenshot: grabs the visible page. You can draw on it or highlight areas.
    • Record video: records the tab while you reproduce the problem. Click stop when done.
    Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+C screenshot · Alt+Shift+R start recording · Alt+Shift+S stop.
  3. Give it a clear title and describe what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Mention the product/page in the title (e.g. "CRM – invoice list doesn't load") and set a priority if you know it.
  4. Submit. You'll get a link to the report.

Videos are kept for 90 days. Screenshots and the written report stay.

When we release an update

The extension does not update by itself. When the product team announces a new version:

  1. Download the zip again from this page and replace the contents of your chrome-mv3 folder (same location).
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and click the ↻ reload arrow on the Crikket card.

Troubleshooting

Problem Fix
"Please sign in" or sent to a login page Sign in at feedback.thetrybe.xyz in the same Chrome profile, then try again.
Report went to the wrong workspace Switch workspace on the website (step 4) and send it again. Ask the product team to delete the wrong one.
Crikket disappeared from Chrome The folder was moved or deleted. Repeat step 2 with the folder in its new place.
Upload fails Check your connection and retry. If it keeps failing, screenshot the error and send it to the product team.
